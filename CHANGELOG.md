# Changelog

## 0.2.0

A character far ahead now always counts in full up to level 30, however low the rest of the group stands, and a quarter for each level beyond. With a friend at 1 Riding, a 35 counts as 31 instead of 20. The level is the new MinimumCap setting; 0 turns it off. Every player needs this version.
